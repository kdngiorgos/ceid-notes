    $p = -x^3 - 3x^2 - 9x + 1 = f(x)$

    ! NOTE !

    | $x$ | $f(x)$ |
    |---|---|
    | $-3$ | $7$ |
    | $0$ | $1$ |
    | $2$ | $-23$ |

    $p_1(x) = ax + b$

    $y_1 \frac{x-x_2}{x_1-x_2} + y_0 \frac{x-x_1}{x_2-x_1} = 7 \frac{x-0}{-3-0} + 1 \cdot \frac{x+3}{0+3}$

    $= -\frac{7}{3}x + \frac{1}{3}x + 1 = -\frac{6}{3}x + 1 = \boxed{-2x+1}$

    $\left. \begin{array}{c|c|c|c} x & y & \text{Πρώτος βαθμός} & \text{β βαθμός} \\ \hline x_0 & -3 & 7 & \\ x_1 & 0 & 1 & \boxed{f_{0,1} = \frac{y_1-y_0}{x_1-x_0} = \frac{1-7}{0+3} = \frac{-6}{3} = -2} \\ x_2 & 2 & -23 & \boxed{f_{1,2} = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} = \frac{-23-1}{2-0} = \frac{-24}{2} = -12} & \boxed{f_{0,1,2} = \frac{f_{1,2}-f_{0,1}}{x_2-x_0}} \end{array} \right.$

    $= \frac{-12+2}{2+3} = \frac{-10}{5} = -2$

    $p_2(x) = 7 + 2(x+3) + 2(x+3)(x-0)$
    $= 7 - 2x - 6 - 2x^2 - 6x$
    $= 1 - 2x - 2x^2 - 6x = 1 - 8x - 2x^2$

    $\left. \begin{array}{l} \sum_{i=0}^{n} f(x_i) \prod_{\substack{j=0 \\ j \neq i}}^{n} (x-x_j) \\ \text{Newton 1o order} \\ p_1(x) = f_0 + f_{0,1}(x-x_0) \\ p_2(x) = f_0 + f_{0,1}(x-x_0) \\ \quad + f_{0,1,2}(x-x_0)(x-x_1) \\ f_{0,1} = \frac{y_1-y_0}{x_1-x_0} \end{array} \right.$
